National context

NIST NVLAP accredits labs for Asbestos Fiber Analysis (PLM and/or TEM). AHERA requires that accreditation for laboratories that analyze samples from K–12 schools. Homeowners should still use an NVLAP-accredited lab rather than an unsourced kit. EPA’s homeowner page (updated 25 June 2026) is the federal starting point for leave / repair / remove decisions.

Methodology and disclosure · State directory

This site is not a government agency and does not accredit laboratories or license asbestos contractors. Rows are transcribed from official federal and state sources cited on each page. Accreditation, licenses, phones, and expiration dates change. Confirm with the laboratory or contractor and with the issuing agency before you ship a sample or hire work.

EPA states that undamaged asbestos-containing material that will not be disturbed is usually best left alone, and that sampling done incorrectly can be more hazardous than leaving the material alone.
