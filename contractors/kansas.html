Kansas licensed asbestos contractors

Companies licensed by the Kansas Department of Health and Environment to perform asbestos-related work in Kansas. This is the state’s list, in the state’s order — not a ranking, not a “best of,” and not an endorsement.

Source: KDHE Licensed Asbestos Contractors PDF, header dated Updated 08/2026. Retrieved 30 August 2026. 77 rows transcribed. License numbers are unknown — the PDF does not print them. Phones are shown because the PDF printed them.

Read this before you call

Analyze first
EPA says to sample through a trained professional and a laboratory. Do not hire removal because a company answered the phone.
Conflict of interest
EPA: the inspector who assesses the need for work should not be the firm that removes it.
Homeowners
KDHE: work on your own residence is not covered under Kansas or federal asbestos regulations; KDHE still points homeowners to EPA’s asbestos pages. Program phone on that page: 785-296-6024.
Out-of-state addresses
Firms based outside Kansas appear because KDHE licensed them for Kansas projects. Confirm they will take a residential job in your city.

EPA states that undamaged asbestos-containing material that will not be disturbed is usually best left alone, and that sampling done incorrectly can be more hazardous than leaving the material alone.
